INT395 Group 12 Standard

Ethical Charter for AI-Driven Student Retention

The EduPulse Retention Intelligence System is designed exclusively as an early decision-support tool to connect students with proactive resources, advising, and financial counseling. Predictions represent statistical associations derived from historical cohorts; they do not define a student's personal destiny, capacity, or academic worth.

1. Strictly Non-Punitive

Under no circumstances may model predictions be used to revoke scholarships, deny course registration, or penalize students.

2. Voluntary Support Offers

All interventions are presented as voluntary opportunities for tutoring, course workload rebalancing, and financial counseling.

3. Right to Algorithmic Recourse

Every flagged assessment must provide transparent, actionable steps detailing what academic changes lower predicted risk.

Zero-Leakage Decision Boundary

Enforcing realistic early intervention timepoints

After Semester 1

In real-world university operations, predicting student dropout at graduation is useless for retention. To prevent temporal target leakage, EduPulse evaluates students strictly after Semester 1:

  • Included: Demographic traits, admission grades, Semester 1 unit enrollment, Semester 1 unit completions, and Semester 1 grade point averages.
  • Excluded (Future Leakage): Semester 2 final exam grades, graduation credit audits, and final status indicators.

This guarantees genuine early detection when university advisors still have ample time to intervene and assist before Semester 2 withdrawal deadlines.

Calibration & Cost-Sensitive Screening

Why standard 0.50 threshold is inadequate in retention

Threshold = 0.38

In educational retention, the cost of a False Negative (missing a struggling student who subsequently drops out) severely exceeds the cost of a False Positive (offering voluntary advising to a student who passes independently).

By calibrating probabilities via CalibratedClassifierCV(method='sigmoid') and selecting an operational threshold of 0.38:

  • Dropout detection recall increases from 71.1% (at 0.50) to 76.8% (at 0.38).
  • Brier score is minimized to 0.1080, ensuring true probabilistic reliability.
  • Advising caseload stays within institutional capacity limits.
