Executive Mission Summary

University attrition typically peaks during the transition between the 1st and 2nd academic years. Traditional retrospective analysis only identifies dropouts when they fail to re-enroll in year 2. EduPulse evaluates students directly at the end of Semester 1, providing institutional academic advisors with an early, actionable window of intervention.
