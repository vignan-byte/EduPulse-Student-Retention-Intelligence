1. 1st Semester Credit Completion is the Single Leading Indicator

Students completing fewer than 50% of enrolled curricular units in Semester 1 exhibit an empirical dropout rate exceeding 74%. Passing at least 4 units reduces risk below the screening threshold.

2. Financial Standing Strongly Amplifies Dropout Risk

Unresolved tuition balances and debtor status increase predicted dropout probability by ~32 percentage points, regardless of prior admission grades.

3. Scholarship Support Acts as a Robust Protective Factor

Scholarship recipients have a graduation rate of 88.5%, with significantly lower attrition even during academic transition difficulties.
